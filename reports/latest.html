I ran the 3D Edwards–Anderson spin glass — Ising spins with quenched random ±J bonds on a simple-cubic lattice, E = −Σ J_ij s_i s_j — across lattice sizes L = [6, 8, 10, 12] on one shared temperature ladder [0.40, 1.60] straddling T_SG ≈ 0.95, averaged over 500 disorder realizations with two replicas each for the overlap q = (1/N) Σ s_i^α s_i^β. Unlike the 2D glass (M11, which orders only at T = 0), the 3D glass has a genuine finite-temperature transition, and its fingerprint is the disorder-averaged Binder cumulant crossing across sizes. Parallel tempering (mean swap acceptance 0.53) equilibrates the cold rungs. Wall time: 2140s.

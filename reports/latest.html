The XY model has NO long-range order at any T > 0 (Mermin–Wagner), so ⟨|m|⟩ is not an order parameter; its transition is the topological Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless vortex-unbinding at T_BKT ≈ 0.8929.

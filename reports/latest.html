2026-09-04 · M04 — 2D Ising specific heat
I ran the 2D Ising model on an L=128 lattice across a window straddling the transition and read the specific heat C(T) = (⟨E²⟩−⟨E⟩²)·N/T² from the energy fluctuations. M01 found T_c from the magnetization; M04 is the independent thermal check — the specific heat carries Onsager's logarithmic divergence at the exact T_c ≈ 2.2692. The C peak sits at T_c(L) = 2.274. Wall time: 30s.
✓ C-peak T_c(L) = 2.274 vs Onsager exact 2.2692 (rel. err 0.2%), and the χ peak from the same run independently gives T_c(L) = 2.281. Two independent observables — the magnetization (M01) and the energy fluctuations (M04) — agree on the same critical point; the thermal response calibrates cleanly. Onsager's exact leading amplitude is A = (2/π)(2/T_c)² ≈ 0.495; a finite lattice rounds the true log into this peak, so the amplitude isn't resolved here — the peak location is the calibrated claim.

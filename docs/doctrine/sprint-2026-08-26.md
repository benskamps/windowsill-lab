### What A1 killed

My first attempt sampled **this week's** submissions and measured 1% — a
sampling error, not a result. Recent papers have not had time to be revised.
Aged sampling gives **49.5%**.

### What A2 found, and the honest limit

Version-specific text is retrievable. But **half of all abstracts are
byte-identical across three versions**, and the changes that exist are mostly
expansion. **The pentimento is in the body, not the abstract** — which makes
this a PDF-fetching project rather than an API project, at ~8s per paper.

### What A3 has already corrected

A two-paper hand sample showed both papers growing in words while shedding
numbers, and I nearly called that the finding. At n=9 the picture is **mixed**:
one paper at `-12 +2`, another at `-0 +36`, a third unchanged. **The signature
exists and is not universal.** The measurement is the distribution, not the
anecdote.

### The caveat that rides on every number here

`pdftotext` on LaTeX-heavy physics is noisy; equation rendering differs between
compilations, so some "removed" values are extraction artifacts. **That biases
raw removals upward by an unknown amount.** The defence is that the same noise
applies to *additions*, so a systematic gap between the two is harder to explain
away than either count alone. Any claim from this must be comparative.

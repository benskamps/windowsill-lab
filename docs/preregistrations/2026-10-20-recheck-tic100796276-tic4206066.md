## Tests on TIC 100796276, fixed now

Run in this order. Each one names its pass/fail line before the data are seen.

**T1 — What this lab's survey would have done.** Run the existing per-sector
`a04.blind_search` (0.5–15 d, MIN_TRANSITS = 3) on each sector. Expected,
stated now: it cannot report 18.15 d *by construction*. Reported as the
instrument's blind spot, not as evidence about the star.

**T2 — Single events, no period.** Run `lab.a05_mono.search_single` (the lab's
single-transit scan with its brightening null) on every sector. List every dip
with SNR ≥ 9 (`SNR_REPORT`), plus every dip with SNR ≥ 7 within ±0.5 d of one
of his six claimed times. *Agrees with him* means each of his six times has a
dip within ±1 h of his time. A claimed time with data but no dip ≥ 7 is a
*disagreement*. A claimed time with no data is *not tested*.

**T3 — Period, no prior.** BLS on the stitched multi-sector series, P ∈ [1, 40] d,
frequency grid fine enough for the full baseline (phase drift < ¼ of a 1-h box
across the baseline), durations {1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4} h. Report the top five
peaks and their SDE. *Agrees* means the top peak is within 0.01 d of 18.152 d
or at an integer ratio (½, ⅓, 2×) that the per-event list in T2 resolves to
18.152 d. Also reported: the peak height and rank at SPOC's 11.47 d.

**T4 — At his period.** Fold at P = 18.15219, T0 = BTJD 2036.9763:
- **Per-event mid-times** by a box χ² scan (±3 h, 1-min step, his 2.4-h
  duration) on each covered epoch. O−C against his ephemeris. *Agrees* means
  every |O−C| ≤ 15 min (his claim is 5 min; 15 min is three times his figure,
  so a single-box fit on one event can be noisier without counting against him).
- **The two predicted epochs** (2025-07-13, 2026-06-05): |O−C| against the
  prediction from his 2020-07-06 + 2023-07-18 pair alone, reported in minutes.
- **SPOC's 11.47 d.** Anchored on the 2020-07-06 event, measure the dip depth
  at +11.47 d and +22.94 d within S27. *Agrees with his account* means neither
  of those epochs shows a dip at ≥ 5σ that is consistent with the primary's depth.

**T5 — Odd/even.** `lab.a05_fold.odd_even_fold` at his ephemeris, half-window
= one transit duration in phase. The lab's own gate: verdict
`eclipsing-binary-odd-even` if both parities are ≥ 5σ and differ by
≥ 5σ (`a04.ODD_EVEN_SIGMA`). Also run `a05_fold.p2_fold` (the 2P fold) under
its own constants.

**T6 — Secondary eclipse and the surface-brightness bound.** Depth at phase 0.5
by `measure_dip` (his duration). Then a ceiling on a secondary at *any* phase:
scan 2.4-h boxes over the whole fold and take max(depth, 0) + 3σ at the deepest.
The ceiling is the larger of the two. The bound is `ceiling / primary depth` on
the companion-to-host surface-brightness ratio. The companion temperature
cut-off comes from `band_ratio` in `scripts/tic374861595_secondary_limit.py`,
reused unchanged, with T_host = 3,490 K. Both response stand-ins are run and
the warmer cut-off is adopted. *Applicable* only if the bound is < 1. *A
stellar companion of the target as the eclipser is excluded* if the cut-off is
below 2,300 K (the §8 census line). Stated now: this bound holds only if the
eclipse is on the target. It says nothing about a blend.

**T7 — Depth across sectors.** `measure_dip`-style box depth per sector at his
ephemeris. χ² against a constant. *Consistent* if p > 0.01 and no sector sits
more than 3σ from the weighted mean. CROWDSAP is reported per sector beside
each depth.

**T8 — Whose light is it (sky gates).** These are the lab's existing gates,
unchanged:
- `a05_sky.resolve_neighbours`, then `a05_sky.flux_budget` with the PDCSAP
  depth and CROWDSAP (per sector, median reported), then
  `a05_sky.neighbour_crosscheck` through `sky_catalog_lookup`.
- `a05_vetting.centroid_shift` (MOM_CENTR minus POS_CORR) at his ephemeris,
  pooled over sectors in pixel units per sector. Run twice: (i) **as the lab
  runs it** — VET_WINDOW_PHASE = 0.03 is ±13 h at 18 d, so the in-transit mean
  is diluted about elevenfold, which biases the test towards *no shift*; it is
  reported but cannot clear anything; (ii) **duration-matched** — in-transit
  |Δt| < T14/2, local out-of-transit T14/2 < |Δt| < 3·T14/2, the same
  bootstrap-over-events and the same CENTROID_SIGMA = 3 / MIN_OFFSET = 0.5 px
  constants. Variant (ii) is the graded one.
- `a05_physical.companion_radius` from the PDCSAP depth and the header RADIUS.

**What cannot be tested here, stated now:** an unresolved companion inside
~1 pixel that the TIC does not list. TESS photometry from this lab cannot
exclude it, and neither can his. Only high-resolution imaging or RVs can.
Pixel-level difference imaging (his "within ~5″") is not in this lab's toolkit
and is **not reproduced**.

## Tests on TIC 4206066, fixed now (if time allows)

The same machinery at each published ephemeris, in this order:
T1 per-sector `a04.blind_search` on every SPOC 2-min sector (3.18 d is inside
the survey's range; *agrees* means a top peak within 1 % of 3.182785 d or an

and the per-sector SNR is reported so that a reader can see that.

## Vocabulary and what each outcome means

The lab's machine vocabulary (`lab.a05_vocab`) applies. The re-check reports,
per test, **agrees / disagrees / not tested**. The overall disposition is one
machine word:

| outcome | disposition | reading |
|---|---|---|
| every applicable test agrees and no gate fires | `lead-awaiting-human-review` | an independent instrument reproduces his signal and his ephemeris; it stays *his* candidate, and the open alternative (an unresolved companion) is unchanged |
| a lab gate fires (odd/even, secondary, centroid, blend, companion size) | that gate's word | the named refutation, with the numbers |
| his events are not found where data exist | `low-significance` | this instrument does not reproduce the signal |
| fewer than two of his events covered by data this lab can read | `insufficient-coverage` | no verdict |

Words that do not appear anywhere in the report: *planet* as a status,
*confirmed*, *validated*, *verified*. "Candidate" appears only as his word for
his object.

## Amendment 1 — committed after the MAST product list, before any flux

The product list (metadata only, no FITS opened) shows, for TIC 100796276:
SPOC 2-min **S27 only**; TESS-SPOC FFI **S27 (600 s), S67 (200 s)**; QLP
S27, S67, S94, S104; **no product of any kind for S105**. For TIC 4206066:
SPOC 2-min S32; TESS-SPOC S6 (1800 s), S32 (600 s); QLP S6, S32, S98.

Under the data rule above, his two *predicted* epochs (S94, S104) would be
untestable. So:

- **QLP is admitted for TIC 100796276 S94 and S104 only**, as a second tier.
  The flux column is `KSPSAP_FLUX` with QLP's own quality flag == 0 and the
  same 0.5-d running median applied. QLP results are reported in their own
  rows, labelled `QLP (amendment 1)`, and are never pooled into the
  SPOC/TESS-SPOC numbers for T3, T5, T6 and T7. They count for T2 and T4
  (event presence and timing). The centroid gate (T8) does not run on QLP.
- **S105 (2026-06-23) is not tested.** No archived light curve exists. Building
  photometry from TESScut cutouts is outside this lab's toolkit and is not
  improvised here.
- The TESS-SPOC S27 product duplicates SPOC S27 at lower cadence. It is used
  only as a cross-check on the S27 depth, not as a second independent sector.
- TIC 4206066 is unchanged: SPOC S32 plus TESS-SPOC S6, with S32 TESS-SPOC as
  a cross-check. QLP S98 is not admitted for it. His S98 discovery data
  therefore stay outside this re-check, and that is stated in the report.

## The question

Does TESS sector 96 contain a transiting planet signal that has not been
catalogued?

## The sample

  searched it: every committed hunt receipt is sector 2, 3 or 30. The only

- **n = 500** two-minute-cadence targets from the driver's consistent-hash

- Targets searched in any committed receipt are excluded by

## The decision rule, fixed now

1. **Detection threshold: SDE ≥ 8.0** (`a04.SDE_THRESHOLD`), the committed
   constant, not a free parameter here.
2. **The pooled null it is priced against is declared in the receipt**
   (`pooled_null`, measured at receipt time, never quoted): as of this commit
   **325,000 scramble draws, maximum SDE 8.6499, 13 draws at or above 8.0**.
   The threshold is **below** the null maximum. Consequence, stated in
   advance: a crossing between SDE 8.0 and 8.65 is *inside the null's range*
   — it is priced, dispositioned and reported, and it cannot be promoted on
   the pooled null. At threshold the 95 % upper bound on the false-alarm rate
   is 6.4e-05, so across 500 targets the expected background at threshold is
   **0.032**; a crossing with an empty tail (SDE > 8.65) carries a bound of
   9.2e-06 and an expected background of **0.0046**.
3. **Per-target FAP** from the dual-scheme permutation null, graded on the
   conservative empirical bound `(1+k)/(B+1)`, exactly as every committed
   receipt.
4. **The machine cannot emit `planet`.** The terminal machine state is
   `lead-awaiting-human-review`; everything else in
   `a05_vocab.MACHINE_DISPOSITIONS` is a refutation or a catalogue match.
5. **Catalogue cross-check runs at report time, never in the search.** A TOI
   match with disposition FP is `toi-known-fp` — not a recovery, not a lead.
6. **Controls** (uniformity, epoch-scramble placebo, budget share) must pass
   or the receipt is refused — by `to_report` at the run, and now also by the
   publish gate, so a failed control cannot reach the ledger by either door.

## What each outcome means, agreed in advance

| Outcome | Verdict for U-A01 |
|---|---|
| One or more `lead-awaiting-human-review` | **unresolved** — a lead is not a planet; it goes to a human, and the entry records that the sector produced a candidate the catalogue does not carry |
| Every crossing refuted or catalogue-matched | **killed** — sector 96 holds no uncatalogued transit this instrument can see, at this sample and this threshold |
| Zero crossings above threshold | **killed**, same reading, with the completeness caveat below |
| Controls fail, or the budget stops the run mid-slice | **no verdict** — the run is withheld and this attempt does not count |

**An empty shelf is a result.** G01 says in its own text that `killed` or
`unresolved` satisfies it exactly as well as `supported`. Nothing here is
optimised for finding something.

## Boundaries, stated before the data

- A **sample** of sector 96, not the sector. No completeness or
  occurrence-rate statement is implied by anything this run produces.
- A lead is a lead. No discovery is claimed, nothing is submitted to ExoFOP,
  and the machine's own vocabulary forbids the word `planet`.
- Absence of a detection in 500 targets is not absence in the sector.
- Depths are reported and never graded — the running-median detrend biases
  them low for long transits.
- **Publication is separately held.** Ben's 2026-09-09 hold on all windowsill
  publication is in force. The receipt this run produces lands on a branch;
  whether it reaches `pot.json` on `main` is his call, not this document's.

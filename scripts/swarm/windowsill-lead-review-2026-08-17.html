Reading the bench — what each axis means

Secondary eclipse

A real eclipsing binary usually shows a dip at phase 0.5 (the companion passing behind). All three secondaries sit below 3σ — no secondary detected. That argues against a hot EB, but a grazing or low-mass companion can still hide here. Necessary, not sufficient.

Self-injection control

The pipeline re-injects each candidate's own depth × period and tries to recover it. All three come back recovered = yes at both epochs — the detection isn't a sensitivity fluke. A positive control, not evidence of a planet.

Odd / even fold

The one the machine won't call. If odd-numbered transits are deeper than even (or vice-versa), the "planet" is really an EB at twice the period. Open each dossier and eyeball the two folds — they should match. This is the heart of the green-leaf decision.

SDE margin & depth

All three clear the floor only narrowly (+0.02 to +0.42). Depth is the other tell: 4.9% and 5.3% are deep — plausible on a small star, suspicious on a sun-like one. 1.5% is hot-Jupiter scale. Depth needs stellar radius to interpret; that context isn't in the receipt.

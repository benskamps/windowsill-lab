# Tracks — what each one is FOR

> `MILESTONES.md` is the ladder: rung by rung, what was measured and what was
> refused. This file is the **destination**. A ladder without a summit is a
> to-do list, and a to-do list cannot tell you when to stop climbing or whether
> the next rung is worth the electricity.
>
> Every track declares two things and nothing else, because a goal that needs a
> page is not yet a goal:
>
> * **Goal** — the thing this track exists to be able to do.
> * **Arrived when** — the observable that would let a stranger agree we got
>   there. Not a feeling. A measurement, or a refusal we can point at.
>
> Read by `lab frontier`, which reports any track still missing a goal rather
> than quietly scoring it as fine. Drafted 2026-08-24; every line here is a
> proposal until Ben rules on it.

## Track M — physics on a lattice

**Goal.** Reproduce the canonical results of statistical mechanics from scratch on one engine, including the ones that are genuinely hard, and know the difference between a result and a number.

**Arrived when.** Every promoted rung's headline is re-derived by its own checker from committed bytes, the hard cases (spin glass, KPZ, directed percolation) sit inside their published bars, and each one states the size of its own uncertainty rather than three decimal places of false precision.

## Track K — coherence

**Goal.** Measure synchronisation the way the literature argues about it, and be able to settle a disagreement rather than join one.

**Arrived when.** K03 returns a verdict on Daido vs Hong — including the verdict "this instrument cannot resolve it", stated with the linearity gate's refusals as the evidence.

## Track C — compute & number theory

**Goal.** Prove the machine can carry an exact computation to a number nobody has to trust it on, because the answer checks itself.

**Arrived when.** Every rung's result is verifiable by a second, independent method — a digit extraction cross-checked against a different series, a residue re-derived by a different identity — so the receipt needs no faith at all.

## Track A — astronomy from open archives

**Goal.** Run a blind planet search that can price its own false alarms, and be honest enough about what it cannot see that a professional would recognise the boundary.

**Arrived when.** A fresh sector is searched with the refutation battery committed BEFORE the data is opened, every threshold crossing carries its own measured false-alarm probability, and the shelf either holds leads that survived adversarial review or is empty with every exit named.

## Track I — the machine as instrument

**Goal.** Turn this box's own hardware into a detector whose noise is characterised well enough that a signal would be believable.

**Arrived when.** A capped sensor's dark noise is measured, hot pixels are separated from transient events by a stated rule, and the run refuses to claim a cosmic ray without the exposure metadata and controls that claim would require.

## Track P — the folding problem

**Goal.** Fold sequences whose right answer this lab can prove for itself, and push the chain length up until proving becomes impossible — then say so instead of guessing.

**Arrived when.** The search recovers exhaustively-proven ground states at every length where enumeration is affordable, and longer chains are reported as best-found in a way that never pools an unproven optimum with a proven one.

## Track B — donate cycles (BOINC)

**Goal.** Contribute compute to somebody else's science, and be clear that this is participation rather than measurement.

**Arrived when.** Validated work units are credited and archived. **This track can never earn a green leaf the way the others do** — its deliverable is a credit record from a pipeline this lab does not run, so there is no observable here to re-derive and no checker that could. That is a fact about the track, not a failure of it.
